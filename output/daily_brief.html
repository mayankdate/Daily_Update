Iron & SweatToday's Workout

  • Rowing Machine

    20–30 min reps Cardio
    Full Body · Upper Back · Quads
    Progression: Damper 3–5. Intervals: 30 s hard / 90 s easy, start at 6 rounds. Too hard if your back rounds or your split time climbs each round. Add a round per week up to 10, or hold a 2–3 s faster split.

  • Incline Treadmill Walk

    30–45 min reps Cardio
    Full Body · Glutes · Calves
    Progression: Effort comes from incline, not speed. Too hard if you need the handrails — drop the incline instead. When you can hold a conversation the whole way, add 1% incline or 0.2 km/h.

  • Stationary Bike

    30–45 min reps Cardio
    Full Body · Quads · Glutes
    Progression: Swap-in for the rower or treadmill. Resistance is right when you can just about speak in short sentences during intervals. Add one resistance level when a session feels easy throughout.
